FERNHILL COOPERATIVE GRANTS PROGRAM
Rules for the 2025 funding cycle
Published 2 January 2025

These rules apply to every applicant in the 2025 cycle. Read each section
carefully; late material is not accepted unless a section says otherwise.

Section 1. Letter of intent
Applicants must send a one-page letter of intent describing the project.
Letters must be received no later than 14 March 2025.

Section 2. Full application
Invited applicants submit the full application through the online form.
The full application is due by 2025-04-30 at 17:00 local time.

Section 3. Budget revisions
If program staff request a revised budget, the applicant must reply within
10 business days of the request.

Section 4. Reference letters
Two reference letters must reach the program office on or before
April 15, 2025.

Section 5. Interim report
Funded projects file a short interim report, due on 30 September 2025.

Section 6. Final report and receipts
The final report, with copies of all receipts, must be filed no later
than January 31, 2026.

Section 7. Appeals
An applicant may appeal a funding decision within 21 days of the
decision notice.

Section 8. Site visit availability form
Shortlisted applicants return the site visit availability form by
2025-04-15.

Contact
The program office is closed from 24 December 2025 to 2 January 2026.
