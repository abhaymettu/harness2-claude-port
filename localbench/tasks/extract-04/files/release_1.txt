NORTHGATE FERRY LINES
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, 3 March 2025

Northgate Ferry Lines announces leadership appointments

Northgate Ferry Lines today announced three changes to its leadership team.

Imelda Corran, currently Director of Operations, has been appointed Chief
Operating Officer, effective April 1, 2025. Corran has led daily sailings
across the network for six years.

Bastian Rook will join Northgate Ferry Lines from outside the company as
Chief Financial Officer, effective 14 April 2025.

Sunniva Hale, currently Head of Fleet Maintenance, has been promoted to
Vice President, Fleet, effective May 5, 2025.

"These appointments set us up for a busy summer season," said the board
chair.

About Northgate Ferry Lines
Northgate Ferry Lines operates passenger and vehicle ferries between six
harbour towns.
