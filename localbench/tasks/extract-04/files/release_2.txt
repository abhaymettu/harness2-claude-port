NORTHGATE FERRY LINES
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, 20 March 2025

Update to leadership announcement

This release updates the announcement of 3 March 2025.

Correction: Bastian Rook will now start as Chief Financial Officer on
7 April 2025, not 14 April 2025 as previously announced.

Northgate Ferry Lines also announced that Teodor Vance, who has served as
Harbor Master since 2019, will succeed Imelda Corran as Director of
Operations, effective 1 April 2025.

All other details of the 3 March announcement are unchanged.
